Questions From Business Owners

Clear, honest answers without technical jargon.

Do my employees need any computer training to use this?

None at all. We design the screens to look like simple forms with big, clearly labeled buttons. If your staff can tap a touch screen or send an email, they will feel completely natural using your custom software in less than 10 minutes.

I own a traditional business (like a hotel). Why not just use Excel or paper notebooks?

Paper notebooks and Excel sheets easily get lost, double-booked, or damaged. A custom software screen gives everyone on your team (reception, cleaning staff, owners) the same updated information on phones, tablets, or computers in real-time — preventing missed bookings and costly mistakes.

What happens if I want to change or add something new next year?

Because we built the code from scratch for you, changing or adding a feature is fast and painless. You just give us a phone call or send a message, and our developers update your system without breaking what you already use.

How much does custom software cost compared to monthly subscriptions?

Commercial software often charges per seat or per room, leading to thousands in recurring monthly costs every single year. With our custom builds, you pay a straightforward price for a system built exclusively for you, resulting in significant long-term savings.
